About

VOXELIUM

A voxel engine built from scratch. No engine, no bloat.

C++20 OpenGL 4.6 CMake
Infinite terrain with no height limit, block breaking and placing with per-block hardness and crack overlays, colorable lamps, world save/load, and a settings menu with actual measured quality presets instead of made-up ones. All 12 items on the original roadmap are implemented and documented with real numbers, not aspirational checkboxes.

What's Actually In It

  • Binary greedy meshing and palette-compressed chunks
  • A jthread-based job system feeding GPU-driven culling (the whole visible world draws in one indirect draw call)
  • Flood-fill RGB lighting
  • A forward HDR pipeline: cascaded shadows, SSAO, volumetrics, bloom, ACES tonemapping
  • Flowing water and falling sand, region-file world persistence

The Audio Is Weirdly Thorough

Sounds resolve by exact block first, then fall back to material, so all 25 lamp block IDs share one sound file instead of shipping 25 copies of the same clip. Fall damage scales with landing weight, being underwater applies an actual sweeping low-pass filter instead of just muffling the volume, and "am I in a cave" is answered by reading the sky-light value the voxel data already has, not by raycasting for it.

House Rules

The project's own docs open with a line worth stealing for basically everything: "Do not invent facts. Do not guess at numbers... A plausible-sounding wrong answer is worse than 'I don't know yet.'" There's also a harden build variant that runs with libstdc++ container assertions on, specifically because it once caught a real data race, plus an offscreen --smoke self-test mode for future CI. The msvc preset exists but is unverified, because "I don't have VS lying around."
