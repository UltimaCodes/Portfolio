Everything I use and what I actually think of it. These are stock photos for now, and where I don't know the brand it's just the closest thing I could find.

cpu

Ryzen 5 5600

Intended to get the 5600X but lowkey got scammed. Still pretty good and doesn't bottleneck, so fire.

★★★★★4/5

Gigabyte RTX 2070 Super OC

Love this GPU, handles almost everything I throw at it. Showing its age though, and 8GB VRAM isn't that good anymore.

★★★★★4/5

MAXSUN B450M Battle-AX

FUCKING HATE THIS MOTHERBOARD. Weird ethernet drivers, had to write my own script to reconnect wifi automatically, took 3 days to update the BIOS because there's no documentation anywhere, and it won't let my RAM run at 3200 CL16, stuck at 3000MHz. Never again.

★★★★★1/5
ram

32GB DDR4 3200MHz

Lucked out and got these for 20K PKR right before the RAM crisis tripled the price. My usage runs high. This was a lifesaver.

OS, workload, and dev drive. Great thing, great speeds, no notes.

1TB SATA

Game and data drive, almost always full. A massive upgrade from the HDD I used to run. No complaints here either.

Hasn't blown up yet and dogwalks my GPU with power to spare. Satisfied.
