Introduction

Hi, I'm Ryaan — a developer, game designer, and robotics enthusiast who's been writing code since I was 9. I started out with Bash scripts and moved on to Python, Pygame, and eventually Unity with C#, where I found my love for building interactive experiences (and occasionally uncovering the weirdest bugs imaginable). I've built everything from text games to experimental prototypes, each one teaching me something new and pushing me to do better. These days, I split my time between leisurely playing games, somewhat cool coding projects, and worrying about my A Levels. I'm proud of how far I've come, and even more excited for what's next.
