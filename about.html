Introduction

Hi, I'm Ryaan. I've been writing code since I was 9, which either makes me a prodigy or just means I've had nine extra years to accumulate bad habits — the jury's still out. Started with Bash scripts because I didn't know any better, graduated to Python and Pygame, and eventually landed in Unity with C#, where I discovered my two true callings: building interactive experiences, and generating bugs so specific they deserve their own Wikipedia page. I've shipped everything from text games nobody played to robots that have, on at least one occasion, tried to set themselves on fire (ask Setup about the battery incidents). These days I split my time between playing games, building things that mostly work, and quietly panicking about my A Levels. Certified early achiever, undiagnosed chaos engineer.
