introduction

I'm Ryaan. I've been writing code since I was 9. I started with Bash scripts because I didn't know any better, moved to Python and Pygame, and ended up in Unity with C#, which is still most of what I do.

I also like hardware. So far that's been a sumo robot, a rover for WRO, boards for a smart cane and a motor controller for our Shell Eco-marathon car, and at least one of those has tried to set itself on fire.

Right now I'm doing A Levels at Nixor College and trying very hard not to get cooked by them. On Team Nixor Sharks I'm the technical lead, the only electrical person and also the driver.
